## What is Musie?

**Musie** is a modern Android music player designed around personal control, visual style, and smooth playback. It brings your music, playlists, lyrics, library, queue, and listening preferences into one focused experience.

The app is made to feel personal: choose your theme, change the player background, customise your home screen, manage your library, and keep your music experience yours.

## Highlights

- Beautiful personalised home screen
- Full-screen now playing experience
- YouTube Music account support
- Search for songs, albums, artists and playlists
- Lyrics with tap-to-change and auto-scroll controls
- Queue, shuffle, repeat and sleep timer
- Like songs and manage your music library
- Downloaded, cached, uploaded and liked playlist sections
- Custom playlist artwork
- Listen Together rooms
- Music recognition shortcut
- Bluetooth auto-resume
- SponsorBlock support
- Modern three-tab navigation: Home, Search and Playlists
- Spotify Mix UI typography
- Custom Musie branding and launcher icon
